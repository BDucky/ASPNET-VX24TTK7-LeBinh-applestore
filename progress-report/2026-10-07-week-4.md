## What landed this week

- **Real email merged** (PR #14 into `dev`, 2026-10-07): registration and
  password-reset codes go out through Gmail SMTP when the settings are
  present on the machine.
- **Testing rules tightened** (PR #15): every task now plans a
  blast-radius table and attack sequences before writing tests, and ends
  with an audit of what was not tested.
- **Admin area** (branch `feat/admin-area`, task 4 of `docs/finish-plan.md`):
  - `/Admin` is an ASP.NET Core Area behind `[Authorize(Roles = "Admin")]`.
    A visitor is sent to sign in; a customer or employee sees the access
    denied page with a link back to the store.
  - A dashboard counts products, products on sale, customers and orders
    straight from the database.
  - The first admin account is created at startup from settings kept in
    user-secrets (never in the repository), through Identity's
    `UserManager`, so the same password rule applies. It is created only
    once and never promotes an existing customer.
  - 26 new tests (138 in total), each behavior a RED commit then a GREEN
    commit. A new rerunnable browser check, `setup/verify-admin/verify.py`,
    passes 31 of 31 at desktop and phone width.
