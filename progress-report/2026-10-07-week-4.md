# Progress report: week 4 (2026-10-07 to 2026-10-13)

Individual project, TVU, Chuyên đề ASP.net, 2026. Repo:
`BDucky/ASPNET-VX24TTK7-LeBinh-applestore`. Started 2026-10-07; updated as
more work lands this week.

## What landed this week

- **Real email merged** (PR #14 into `dev`, 2026-10-07): registration and
  password-reset codes go out through Gmail SMTP when the settings are
  present on the machine.
- **Testing rules tightened** (PR #15): every task now plans a
  blast-radius table and attack sequences before writing tests, and ends
  with an audit of what was not tested.
- **Admin area** (branch `feat/admin-area`, task 4 of `docs/finish-plan.md`):
  - `/Admin` is an ASP.NET Core Area behind `[Authorize(Roles = "Admin")]`.
    A visitor is sent to sign in; a customer or employee sees the access
    denied page with a link back to the store.
  - A dashboard counts products, products on sale, customers and orders
    straight from the database.
  - The first admin account is created at startup from settings kept in
    user-secrets (never in the repository), through Identity's
    `UserManager`, so the same password rule applies. It is created only
    once and never promotes an existing customer.
  - 26 new tests (138 in total), each behavior a RED commit then a GREEN
    commit. A new rerunnable browser check, `setup/verify-admin/verify.py`,
    passes 31 of 31 at desktop and phone width.

- **Shopping cart** (branch `feat/cart`, use cases 11-14):
  - "Add to cart" on the product page posts the chosen colour and region.
    A visitor is asked to sign in first and comes back to the same choice.
  - The cart page changes quantities, removes lines, and shows the
    subtotal; the nav shows how many items are in the cart.
  - Quantities stay between 1 and the stock; sold-out or unpriced choices
    cannot be added; a product taken off sale stays visible in the cart
    with the reason and leaves the subtotal.
  - Two adds at the same moment end in one line (unique indexes added by
    an EF Core migration); a database failure shows a message instead of
    an error page.
  - 46 new tests (184 in total); browser check `setup/verify-cart/verify.py`
    passes 23 of 23.

- **Checkout and vouchers** (branch `feat/checkout`, use cases 15-16):
  - A checkout page fills in the saved address, applies a voucher and
    places the order (cash on delivery); the order page shows it.
  - Vouchers have their own table, following the report's business rule:
    percent or fixed, all or some products, a minimum order, a usage limit
    and a time window. Three demo vouchers are seeded.
  - Placing runs in one database transaction: stock, voucher use and the
    cart change together or not at all. A double submit makes one order;
    a price that changed while the page was open is shown again first.
  - 43 new tests (227 in total); browser check
    `setup/verify-checkout/verify.py` passes 25 of 25.

- **Online payment** (branch `feat/payment`, use case 17, 2026-10-08):
  - Checkout offers cash on delivery, VNPay and MoMo. With no sandbox
    accounts, VNPay and MoMo go through a gateway page simulated inside the
    app, which signs its answers the way VNPay does (HMAC-SHA512).
  - The order page has "Pay now" after a failed or cancelled payment; every
    attempt is kept. An answer that was changed, has the wrong amount, or
    arrives twice cannot mark an order paid or count it twice.
  - The simulator is switched on by configuration and the app refuses to
    start with it in Production.
  - 46 new tests (273 in total); browser check
    `setup/verify-payment/verify.py` passes 16 of 16.

- **Orders after checkout** (branch `feat/orders`, use cases 18-19, 22-24
  and 37, 2026-10-08):
  - Customers have "My orders", can cancel while an order waits for
    confirmation (stock and voucher use come back), and get an email when
    the order is placed and when it ships.
  - Anyone can track an order with its number and the receiver's phone; the
    page shows status and tracking only.
  - Staff (admins and employees) list orders by status and confirm, ship
    with a carrier and tracking number, complete or cancel them. The buttons
    follow one table of rules: an online order needs payment before it is
    confirmed, a delivered cash order counts as paid.
  - Two staff pressing the same button, or a customer cancelling while staff
    confirm, cannot apply a change twice. A cancelled order can no longer be
    paid.
  - 62 new tests (335 in total); browser check `setup/verify-orders/verify.py`
    passes 19 of 19, and the five earlier checks still pass.

- **Admin pages for the catalog, vouchers and accounts** (branch
  `feat/admin-products`, use cases 25-27 and 29-31, 2026-10-08):
  - Admins add and edit products and their variants (price, stock, on
    sale), pick the photo from the shop's own photo library, and take a
    product off sale instead of deleting it, so old orders stay intact.
  - Admins add, edit and delete vouchers; the rules from the report are
    checked, and the use count is never overwritten.
  - Admins give accounts a role (customer, employee, admin); the person's
    open sessions end at once.
  - Two admins editing the same thing, or a sale landing while an admin edits
    stock, cannot overwrite each other.
  - Licensed photos found for Mac mini, iPhone Duo and Apple Watch 12.
  - 63 new tests (398 in total); browser check
    `setup/verify-admin-catalog/verify.py` passes 15 of 15, and the six
    earlier checks still pass.

- **Whole-app review** (2026-10-08): every page checked live again, then a
  security and a correctness review of the whole code. Authorization,
  ownership, anti-forgery and payment signatures came out clean; seven
  issues were fixed with tests, the most important being admin prices typed
  with dots being saved as empty, and the registration code having no limit
  on wrong guesses. 410 tests; all seven browser checks pass.

- **Sales report, Excel export and invoices** (branch `feat/admin-reports`,
  use cases 33-36, 2026-10-08):
  - Staff see revenue, paid orders, items sold and the average order for any
    date range, by day and by product. Only paid orders that were not
    cancelled count, by the day in Vietnam time.
  - The report downloads as a real Excel file and prints (or saves as PDF)
    from a plain print page; customers and staff print invoices.
  - 16 new tests (426 in total); browser check `setup/verify-reports/verify.py`
    passes 14 of 14, including a downloaded workbook and a real PDF.
