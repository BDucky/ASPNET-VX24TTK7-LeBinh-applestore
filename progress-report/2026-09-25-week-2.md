## Still open

Gaps from the side-by-side check that can be fixed with current data:
- Homepage ends in a 2x2 grid of half-width banners on rauvang; ours
  stacks every banner full width (page 5979px tall against 3753px).
- Carousel arrows and dots are visible on ours, hidden on rauvang.
- Model row tiles: rauvang uses small line icons (44x60, 13px grey
  labels, 84px tiles); ours uses photos (64x64, 120px tiles) and leaves a
  gap under the nav.
- Variant page: price 24px in rgb(230,8,52) on rauvang, 33.6px in
  rgb(239,68,68) on ours; rauvang's labels are yellow on black.

Gaps that need data or a decision first:
- **Variant data.** Every product has one photo and one variant (AirTag
  has two). rauvang shows 8 cards for the same model (4 storage sizes by
  2 regions), with colour swatches, 5 photos, and names like "256GB (VN)"
  where ours shows the SKU code. Needs real prices per tier.
- Model order: rauvang lists newest first; ours follows database order.
  Needs an order field.
- Nav: rauvang has Installments, Used, Repair, and Promotions pages; we
  have none of these.
- Prices are in USD. rauvang shows VND.
- Carried from week 1: instructor collaborator acceptance pending, branch
  protection needs repo admin, and M1 register/verify-OTP UI not started.
