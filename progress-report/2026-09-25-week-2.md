**2026-09-23 to 2026-09-25: branch `feat/catalog-vn-expansion`, committed and opened as a pull request into `dev`.**
- Six seed migrations with the current apple.com/vn lineup (iPhone 18 Pro
  Max and 17e, MacBook Neo, iMac, iPad mini, Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4,
  AirPods, Apple TV 4K, accessories), each with a verified photo.
- Storefront rebuilt to follow rauvang.com's page flow, after a second
  live pass through its home, category, model, and variant pages on
  2026-09-25:
  - Home: a rotating top banner, a store-policy banner, then one
    full-width banner per product line, each linking to its model page.
  - Category page: only the strip of models in that category plus the
    policy banner. The product grid now appears only for search results.
  - Model page: the same model strip above a grid of variant cards.
  - Variant page: photo on the left; price, warranty, benefits, and
    product information on the right.
  - Nav with the active category marked, a search field that opens under
    the bar, a three-column footer, and a back-to-top button.
  - Kept from the earlier design: the dark gradient background, smooth
    scrolling, reveal-on-scroll, parallax, the custom cursor, and the 3D
    scene (now behind the top banner).
- Test-first: `ProductDetail` and `ProductVariantDetail` now carry the
  category slug, the model and variant pages load their model strip, and
  the homepage builds its banners from a curated list and skips any
  product that is missing. 38 of 38 tests pass. Each behavior is a RED
  commit then a GREEN commit, and every RED commit was checked on its own
  to build and fail only the new assertions.
- Side-by-side check against rauvang.com (2026-09-25): the same four pages
  on both sites, captured with Playwright at 1440x900 and measured (nav,
  model row, cards, title and price sizes and colours). It found 15 gaps
  still open, listed below. The dark gradient is a deliberate difference.

**2026-09-25 to 2026-09-29: branch `feat/variant-sync` (catalog synced with rauvang.com), committed and opened as a pull request.**
- Crawled rauvang.com's iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Watch sections (36 models,
  230 configuration cards, 797 colour and region prices in VND, recrawled
  2026-09-29) and its AirPods and accessory lists. The raw crawl, a dated snapshot, and the
  scripts that turn it into an EF Core data migration live in
  `setup/rauvang-sync/`, so the sync can be rerun.
- Decisions made with the user: full sync with prices in VND; no-price
  items show "Contact for price" (null, never 0); one page per
  configuration with colour and region chosen on the page; varied demo
  stock numbers, since rauvang publishes none.
- Schema: `ProductVariant.Price` and `Product.BasePrice` nullable,
  `Product.SortOrder` added (documented in `docs/data-model.md`).
- Model pages now show one card per configuration. The variant page has
  colour and region choices that swap price, SKU, and stock in place.
- Photos: 12 new licensed Wikimedia Commons photos, one per model, credited
  on the Credits page. rauvang's own photos are not used.
- Test-first: configuration grouping, configuration lookup, price sorting
  with "Contact for price" last, VND formatting, URL slugs. 53 of 53 tests pass.
- Photo fix: every product now shows only a photo of its exact model
  (verified on Wikimedia Commons). 6 wrong or hotlinked photos were replaced,
  3 older-generation stand-ins removed, and 8 models with no verified photo
  show "Photo coming soon" instead of an empty box. Audit: 60 pages, 663
  images, 0 broken, 0 loaded from another server.
- Footer contact and project text updated: binhl030199@tvu-onschool.edu.vn,
  subject "Chuyên đề ASP.net".
- Checked live against rauvang.com with Playwright on 2026-09-29
  (`setup/rauvang-sync/verify.py`): 4 of 4 category model rows identical in

  region prices identical. rauvang changes prices during the day (on
  2026-09-25 it lowered MacBook Air prices within an hour), so the snapshot
